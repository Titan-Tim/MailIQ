Mail-IQ

Scan the post in. Send personalised mail out. Always know what came back.

One platform for a whole organisation's mail. Inbound: scan the day's post once and it's split, classified, routed to the right person and filed into your systems. Outbound: turn one document into a personalised mailshot delivered by post, email or SMS — with a closed loop that tells you exactly who has responded.

01 What it does

Your mailroom, both directions, in one place

Six things Mail-IQ does out of the box. Everything after this is a walk through the screens that deliver them and how you switch each one on.

▤

Digital mailroom

Scan a batch of incoming post once — Mail-IQ splits it at separator sheets, reads each document, classifies it, and routes it to the right mailbox.

⤓

Files into your systems

Legal letters filed into Proclaim named by matter reference; invoices forwarded to your AP system — read off the document, no re-keying.

◈

Personalised campaigns

One document → a personalised copy for every recipient, each addressed and stamped with a unique QR — a mailshot in one click.

⇆

Post, email & SMS

Each recipient is reached the way they prefer — a letter in the post, a tracked email, or a text with a secure link — decided from your data.

↩

Closed-loop returns

When a document comes back — scanned in by post, or uploaded to the recipient portal — Mail-IQ registers who responded. Reconciliation, automatic.

◑

Cloud or on-premise

Run it hosted, or entirely inside your own network for compliance — with a signed licence and per-module control.

03 Inbound · mailboxes & routing

To the right team — and only the right team

Mailboxes are the internal destinations post is routed to: shared team boxes and private personal ones. Routing rules decide, by document type and keyword, which box each item lands in — and anything unmatched waits safely in triage rather than going astray.

09 The closed loop

Know exactly who has responded — on paper or online

The part that sets Mail-IQ apart. A returned document — a ballot, a form, a renewal — comes back either way: posted back and scanned through the inbound mailroom, or uploaded to the portal. Both land in the same place, so you can see at a glance who's replied and chase only who hasn't.
